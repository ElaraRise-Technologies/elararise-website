What we build

One foundation. Unlimited intelligence at the edge.

01 — Vision AI

Cameras become intelligence.

NVIDIA Jetson edge devices run fine-tuned computer-vision models against live camera feeds — detecting anomalies, counting, grading, and alerting in real time. Fifty-millisecond inference. Zero cloud dependency for the critical path. ElaraRise Vision AI analyzes objects and site operations only. The platform does not perform facial recognition, biometric identification, or tracking of individuals.

02 — WhatsApp Commerce

Ordering at every counter.

Customers order, receive receipts, and confirm delivery through the channel they already use every day. No new app. No friction. Commerce embedded into the conversations that already happen.All transactions are processed via the official WhatsApp Business Platform. ElaraRise does not handle payments or store card details on this website.

03 — Vertical Platforms

Twenty-two verticals, one device.

Quick-service, retail, hospitality, agriculture, logistics, banking, and beyond. Each vertical inherits the foundation — and adds only the intelligence specific to its operators.

Why ElaraRise

The platform is the product. Verticals are the revenue.

I.

Engineered where we operate.

Most AI platforms are retrofitted from markets where bandwidth is free and power is constant. ElaraRise is designed for East Africa first — where surge protectors are mandatory, where mobile money matters more than card rails, and where the customer’s first interface is already WhatsApp.

II.

One device. Every future door open.

A single Jetson device at each site serves Vision AI today, and every future vertical tomorrow. The architecture is the insurance — new capabilities ship as software updates, never as new hardware installations.

III.

Intelligence from day one.

Our financial and operational intelligence tier activates with a decade of real operating data — not a cold start. The platform reasons from context, not from default benchmarks drawn from unrelated markets.

IV.

IP owned. Stack discipline.

ElaraRise owns one hundred per cent of its IP from day one. Our technology stack is deliberately narrow and deliberately fixed. Every external dependency — AI model, storage provider, payment gateway — sits behind an interface, replaceable without a refactor.
